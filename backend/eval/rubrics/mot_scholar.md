# Rubric — MOT Theory Fidelity (L8, also L3 spot-checks)

Judges whether the Strategist (and, spot-checked, the MOT Lens) applies
Management-of-Technology frameworks **correctly and predictively** — not as
decorative labels. Doctrine reference: [`docs/MOT_Framework_Library.md`](../../../docs/MOT_Framework_Library.md).

## How to score — per signal
Score **0–3**:
- **0** — framework named but wrong for the situation, or misapplied (decorative).
- **1** — correct framework, but shallow (a label, no mechanism).
- **2** — correct framework + correct mechanics + right value-capture logic.
- **3** — all of 2 **and** genuinely *predictive* (says what happens next / who
  captures value / what to do), with the framework's **preconditions evidenced** in
  the cited sources.

For each signal record: framework(s) claimed → score → the discriminator you used →
any misapplication.

## Framework checks (is the RIGHT tool used RIGHTLY?)
- **S-curve / maturity (Schilling; Anderson–Tushman).** Stage cues match reality;
  hype ≠ maturity; performance limits / discontinuities reasoned. *Fail:* calling
  something "mature" on a single splashy launch.
- **Dominant design & ferment (Utterback–Abernathy).** Many entrants → shakeout →
  design locks; competition shifts **performance → cost/scale**. *Fail:* declaring a
  "dominant design" off one deal, or with players still proliferating.
- **Diffusion & the chasm (Rogers; Moore).** Correct adopter categories; the chasm
  is visionaries→**pragmatists**, who buy on value/whole-product/infrastructure, not
  vision. *Fail:* labeling early-pilot volumes as "early majority."
- **Standards battles (van de Kaa).** Requires an **installed base / network
  effects / sponsor coordination**; identifies the barrier correctly. *Fail:*
  "standards battle" where there's no network effect or lock-in mechanism.
- **Appropriability & complementary assets (Teece).** Value accrues to the owner of
  the **scarce, co-specialized complementary asset**; regime is tight vs weak.
  *Fail:* conflating *installed base* with *complementary asset*; assuming the
  innovator captures value in a weak-appropriability regime.
- **Platforms & envelopment (Gawer).** Multi-sidedness / platform envelopment used
  only where a real platform dynamic exists.
- **Real options & investment appraisal (Berk–DeMarzo).** Staged commitment vs full
  commitment framed as **option value under uncertainty**; not just "they invested."

## Cross-cutting checks
- **Predictive vs post-hoc.** Does the read say what the theory implies *next*, or
  just relabel what already happened? (Post-hoc labeling caps at 1.)
- **Evidence supports preconditions.** The framework's assumptions (network effect,
  scarce asset, pragmatist buyers…) must appear in the cited items — not asserted.
- **Correct axis.** Maturity (tech lifecycle) vs adoption (market diffusion) not
  conflated.
- **Overreach.** Assertive verbs ("wins", "confirms") only when evidence warrants;
  otherwise flag and dock.

## Aggregate
Mean signal score → **Green ≥ 2.3 · Amber 1.5–2.3 · Red < 1.5**, plus a list of any
outright misapplications (each is a fix item regardless of the mean).
